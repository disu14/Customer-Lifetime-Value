Customer results

Column meanings:
  • Customer ID: Unique customer identifier from your CSV.
  • Frequency: Number of repeat purchases (after the first).
  • Recency (days): Days between the last and first purchase, up to observation end.
  • T (days): Customer age in days (first purchase to observation end).
  • Avg order value: Average revenue per transaction.
  • Pred. purchases (30d): Expected number of purchases in the next 30 days.
  • Probability alive (%): Chance the customer is still active.
  • CLV (12 months): Expected revenue over the next 12 months.
